## Refine and complete your charter
- **What We Own. What this team owns.:** We own the systems that get a foreman's RFI to the right approver and the answer back to him, whatever the signal on site.
- **What is out of scope.:** Capture UX and the mobile experience, Enterprise web features and renewal commitments, and Deferred functions, for everyone, this phase.
- **Cross-boundary decisions that need a joint call.:** Changes to the RFI data contract or APIs, Approval-chain configuration readiness across the 38 accounts, Any engineer reassigned across teams for one sprint or longer, Enterprise requests that touch routing logic, approval chains, or notifications, Rollout beyond pilot projects, and Adding superintendent-facing surfaces.
- **How We Decide. Who decides feature and scope calls.:** The strategy is the tiebreaker, and the current allocation holds until a change is decided in writing, so engineering committed to Now Bets 1 through 3 is fixed for the quarter and moves only through escalation. Foundations decides routing, sync, and notification architecture and its own sequencing; PM 1 decides capture UX and mobile flows; PM 2 decides enterprise work that fits within PM 2's capacity; the Head of Product decides anything that pulls engineers off Bets 1 through 3; and leadership, CPO, CRO, and CEO together, decides anything that reopens a deferred function or changes team composition.
- **How cross-team conflicts escalate.:** Any cross-team request gets logged with its customer, renewal date, ARR at risk, and engineering weeks, then goes to the PM triad plus eng lead within 2 business days, where the requesting PM brings the revenue case and the affected PM brings which bet slips and by how much. If the triad can't reach consensus that day, the Head of Product with the Eng Director decides within 3 business days, in writing, naming what slips, and that role is the answer to who protects the field bet. Anything that reopens a deferred function or changes team composition skips the triad and goes to leadership, CPO, CRO, and CEO, within 5 business days, where sales holds a decision seat. The ceiling is 10 business days end to end, and while an escalation is open no engineer moves and sales commits no timing to the customer, so delay costs the requester rather than the field team.
- **Who resolves escalations from outside the team.:** Outside requests enter through the PM who owns the surface being asked about, get logged with the same four facts, and then resolve at whichever level the ask belongs to: the PM triad if it needs committed field engineering, the Head of Product if the triad can't agree, leadership if it reopens a deferred function or touches team composition.

## Show and swap your team charter
- **Where does the charter leave room for interpretation that could cause a conflict?:** The biggest gap is that "fits within PM 2's own capacity" and "needs engineering committed to Bets 1 through 3" are the same request seen from two sides whenever engineers are shared, so PM 2 can honestly decide alone while PM 1 watches capacity disappear.
